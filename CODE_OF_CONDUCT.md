# Code of Conduct

Quappe exists to make disagreement productive. The same values we build into
the product apply to the people building it.

## Our standard

1. **The integrity of a being is untouchable. Always.**
2. **Separate the person from the position.** Critique ideas, never people.
3. **Assume good faith.** Extend it first; earn it back when it's broken.
4. **Minorities deserve a voice and a safe space** — and the majority is still
   the majority. Both are true; hold the tension with care.
5. **Qualify yourself.** Bring reasoning, evidence, and nuance rather than
   volume or status.
6. **Trolls get marked kindly first, firmly later.** Repeated disruption leads
   to being ignored, then excluded.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions,
commit messages, and any Quappe-run community channel — and to public spaces
when an individual is representing the project.

## Not tolerated

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination of any kind.
- Personal attacks, demeaning language, or dehumanising others.
- Exposure of private information without explicit consent.
- Sustained bad-faith disruption after a good-faith warning.

Disagreement, sharp critique of ideas, and strong argument are welcome — that
is the point. Attacking people instead of positions is not.

## Reporting

Report violations by opening a report to the repository admins (GitHub:
"Report content") or, for sensitive matters, via a private
[security advisory](https://github.com/quappe-org/quappe/security/advisories/new)
so it reaches the maintainers privately. Reports are handled confidentially.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, and warn, temporarily block, or
permanently exclude anyone whose behaviour breaches this code. Enforcement aims
to be proportionate and restorative first, protective when it must be.
